Each study draws two independent samples of size n from normal populations with SD = 1 whose means differ by d, then runs a two-sided Welch's t-test (which does not assume equal variances). Everything runs in your browser, so results differ every time.

The idea and the name come from Geoff Cumming. See Cumming, G., & Calin-Jageman, R. (2024). Introduction to the new statistics: Estimation, open science, and beyond (2nd ed.). Routledge.
